## Full Description

### 🎓 Own Your Education
**RealTimeX DeepTutor** is more than just a study tool; it is your private, personalized AI university. Whether you are mastering a new textbook, researching complex topics, or preparing for exams, RealTimeX DeepTutor transforms static documents into an interactive, intelligent learning environment that adapts to you—all running locally on your machine.

### 🚀 Key Features

-   **Massive Document Q&A**: Upload textbooks, papers, and manuals. RealTimeX DeepTutor's **LightRAG** engine allows you to chat with your entire library, delivering precise answers with citations.
-   **Interactive Learning Visualization**: Don't just read—see. RealTimeX DeepTutor generates interactive HTML lessons and visual aids to break down complex concepts into easy-to-understand guides.
-   **Exam Simulation & Practice**: Prepare with confidence. The **Question Generator** can mimic the style of your past exams (Mimic Mode) or create custom quizzes to test your knowledge gaps.
-   **Deep Research Agent**: Go beyond the text. RealTimeX DeepTutor's multi-agent research loop explores topics, connects concepts across disciplines, and synthesizes findings into structured reports.
-   **Intelligent Co-Writer**: Brainstorm, outline, and refine your ideas with an AI partner that understands your specific research context and style.

### 🛡️ Privacy-First: Own Your Knowledge Base
RealTimeX DeepTutor is built on the **"Own Your Data"** philosophy.
-   **Local Storage**: All your knowledge bases, learning sessions, and research artifacts are stored in your local `data/` directory.
-   **Private & Secure**: Your personal learning data never leaves your control. You choose which models to use and where your data lives.

### 🔌 RealTimeX Desktop Integration
Designed to run as a **Local App** within the RealTimeX ecosystem:
-   **Unified AI Access**: RealTimeX DeepTutor leverages the **RealTimeX SDK** to use your configured local models (Ollama, LM Studio) or API keys seamlessly.
-   **Zero Configuration**: No need to manage separate keys or configs; it inherits your RealTimeX environment settings automatically.
